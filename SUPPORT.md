# Support

## The demo

https://demo.shelfaware.net is a free, shared instance of Reginald running on the host's own
spend-capped AI key — you do not need an API key to try it. Because it is shared and the AI calls
cost the host real money, it has daily caps:

- a box-wide limit on how many **new accounts** can be created per day;
- a **per-household** daily allowance of AI calls and tokens;
- a **box-wide** daily cap on AI calls.

The app tells you when you have hit one, and each resets the next day. The numbers are not promised
here because they change; what is promised is that the app will say so rather than fail silently.

Your data stays until you remove it yourself from **Settings → Delete all my data**. Uptime is
best-effort: it is one small droplet looked after by one person, and it is occasionally redeployed.

## Reporting a bug

Use the **🐞 Report a bug** link in the app's footer. It captures the page you were on, your browser
and the last few client-side errors, shows you exactly what it will attach, and lets you drop any
part of it before sending. That is the fastest route, because it arrives with the context.

If you cannot sign in, or the problem is in the code rather than the running app, open a
[GitHub issue](../../issues/new/choose) instead. Please do not paste receipt images or personal data
into a public issue.

## What there is not

There is no support email address, no SLA and no phone number. Issues and in-app reports are read,
and answered, by the maintainer when he is at his desk.
